Three services, one app

Dog walking

A local walker picks your dog up and takes it for a walk - on your schedule. The walker marks the start and end of the walk in the app and can send you photos along the way.

Dog boarding

Your dog stays at the sitter's home while you travel. Each sitter's profile shows the price per night, how many dogs they take, and which sizes.

House sitting

The sitter comes to your home, so your dog keeps its own routine - feeding, walks, and company while you are away. Details are arranged directly with the sitter.

How it works

  1. 1. Download BARK and pick a service. Walking, boarding, or house sitting - plus the dates you need.
  2. 2. Browse profiles in your neighborhood. Prices, bios, reviews from other owners, and a verified badge - all visible before you send a request.
  3. 3. Send a request to the person you like. They see your dog's details and accept if the time works for them.
  4. 4. Chat in the app. Once accepted, you arrange everything in the in-app chat - including a meet and greet before the first booking.

Frequently asked questions

How much does dog walking cost in Belgrade?

Each walker sets their own price, shown on their profile before you send a request. BARK takes no commission - you arrange payment directly with the walker.

Are the dog walkers and sitters vetted?

Walkers and sitters can verify their identity with an ID document, which earns a verified badge on their profile. Owners also leave reviews after every walk or sitting, visible to everyone.

Is the BARK app available in English?

The app is currently in Serbian. The booking flow is simple and visual - pick a service, a date, and a walker - and many walkers and sitters in Belgrade speak English, so communication is usually easy.

What is the difference between dog boarding and house sitting?

With boarding, your dog stays at the sitter's home while you are away. With house sitting, the sitter comes to your home so your dog stays in its own routine. Both options are available in the app, priced per night.

Which areas of Belgrade does BARK cover?

BARK works across Belgrade - you choose your neighborhood in the app and see walkers and sitters who work in your area.

How do I book a dog walker in Belgrade?

Download the BARK app, pick a service and date, browse profiles with prices and reviews, and send a request to the walker you like. Once they accept, you chat in the app to arrange the details.
